Apokalypsis

July 4, 2023

Greek yoghurt,
a white sea, all limewashed,
and
cleansed woollen and
woven
like cumulus clouds
in robes
around the last days.

Breath slow as trumpets voice
in all fur and
time
to the omega ticking
by the seven
that
come and go
delivered
in ink
spread like marbled butter
over
Ionic columns
and further
afield.

Unearthly,
too many eyes
spirited
in wax-red
light;
and the
berries bleed hot,
dripping
to be savoured as
everyone’s vision is
centred.


by Dylan
