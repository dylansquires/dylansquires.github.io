At work there are many cups,\
Each accompanied\
By company name,\
But if mine's there I'm in luck.\
\
Only one cup is orange,\
My favourite colour,\
So I favour it\
For my morning coffee binge.\
\
It must align with my self,\
My identity,\
And I dare not see\
Such that it's not on the shelf.\
\
If it's not there I'll grow pale,\
My heart'll divide,\
For its my divine\
Coffee cup made Holy Grail.\
\
by Dylan
