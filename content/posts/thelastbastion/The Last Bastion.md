A bloke outside the toilets of Wembley\
Chatted to me as the bartender\
Said that'll be ten please\
For a pint that gentrified Camden\
\
He chatted to me about Sam Fender\
As we stood about in the smell of piss\
And he told me of his pride and fandom\
For his local team\
Yet to sell itself to the hell\
That is the national league\
\
So on he went with a poppy on his chest\
About how he'd spent his youth\
Fighting for Thatcher\
Watching his pension get fatter until poof\
And down the sink it all went\
Clogging the pipes mixed with sick\
With his youth eroded bit by bit\
With no pension to prop up his roof\
\
In desperation in search of a hit\
He went out in winter frost\
To watch his local team\
To stand in the cold arms crossed\
They lost 2-0\
And he almost binned the whole thing off\
\
But after the game in the Cotswolds Arms\
He chatted to a bloke outside the toilets\
Who spoke to him about\
How the hell they could have lost\
He said some of his folk are going\
To watch them play away next Saturday\
There's a coach leaving ten o'clock\
\
So off he went and saw Big Ben\
Head it in from outside the box\
Then go on to win it with a last minute pen\
And in the cheers he found the last bastion\
Of British community.\
\
by Dylan
