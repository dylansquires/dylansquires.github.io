Over the back of my hand,\
a bug,\
smaller than a freckle,\
dotted and redotted\
red.\
\
I tried to pick\
the lil’ guy\
up, roll him\
onto another finger,\
but he refused,\
running around,\
away.\
\
So I brushed him\
off,\
and he landed\
on the wooden\
table.\
Still.\
\
I thought I’d killed him,\
I, death-cloaked,\
creaked a bony nail\
towards\
the red\
dot.\
\
Then, in an instance,\
streaked\
across the\
wood,\
the remains.\
\
Others might think\
the mark to be\
the ink\
of a pen,\
but I know\
what happened\
here.\
\
by Dylan
